I am a graduate student in Computer Science at North Dakota State University. My research focuses on natural language processing (NLP), large language models (LLMs), healthcare AI, and knowledge representation. I am particularly interested in building reliable AI systems, developing linguistic resources for low-resource languages, and leveraging AI to support scientific discovery and healthcare research.


This portfolio is under construction. Thanks for visiting.


## Research Interests

  - Natural Language Processing (NLP): Low-resource languages, computational lexicography, word sense disambiguation, and multilingual NLP.

  - AI for Healthcare: LLM-powered healthcare applications, biomedical information retrieval, clinical data analysis, and research assistance.

  - Large Language Models (LLMs): Model evaluation, domain adaptation, retrieval-augmented generation (RAG), and trustworthy AI.

  - AI Agents: Agentic systems, tool use, autonomous research workflows, and intelligent data analysis.

  - Knowledge Representation: Biomedical ontologies, semantic technologies, and cross-dataset harmonization.
